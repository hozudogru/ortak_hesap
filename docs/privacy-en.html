This privacy policy explains what data the Ortak Hesap mobile app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and what rights you have as a user.

Data We Collect

DataPurpose
Email address, passwordAccount creation and sign-in (Firebase Authentication)
Name, nicknameTo identify you to other group members
Group, expense, and payment recordsTo calculate shared expenses and who owes what
Push notification token (FCM token)To send debt reminders and group notifications

The App does not include any third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

Data Storage

All data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication) and transmitted over industry-standard encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Sharing

The group, expense, and payment information you enter is only visible to the other members of the group you belong to. Your data is never shared with or sold to advertisers or other third parties.

Camera Permission

The App requests camera access so you can scan a group's join QR code. The camera is only active while you're using the QR scan screen; no images are saved or sent to any server.

Deleting Your Data

If you'd like to delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email address below; your request will be processed within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Ortak Hesap is not designed for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page.
